# Privacy — Banana Factory Frenzy 0.1.0-beta.1

- **Works offline.** This beta does not use the internet.
- **No data collection.** The app itself contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDK, account system or network telemetry.
- **One permission.** The app requests only Android's vibration permission, used for haptic feedback.
- **Your progress stays on your phone.** Game progress is saved locally on the device.
- **Bug reports are manual.** Because nothing is collected automatically, problems reach us only when you report them through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/sahar4you/banana-factory-frenzy-releases/issues). Please do not include personal information, save files or other sensitive data in a report. Anything you post in an issue is public.
- **Future betas.** Telemetry or crash reporting may be evaluated for a future beta only after it has been disclosed and approved. Any such change will be described here and in the release notes before it ships.
